## North star
Build a direct-to-consumer e-commerce business targeting **EUR 15,000 in monthly gross revenue run rate by 2026-12-31** (49 calendar days from Nov 13; 45-day core execution sprint through Dec 27 plus 4-day year-end review/optimization). This is an ambitious target, not a forecast or guarantee. Distinguish revenue, collected cash, contribution margin and net profit. No advertising spend, purchases, publication or destructive changes without owner approval.
## Initial launch market decision (2026-11-13)
**Spain-first** for initial validation, provisional and reversible after product-level unit economics and supplier checks. Spanish-language creative/support reduces execution friction. Germany and France are research comparators, not simultaneous launch markets. US is deferred. This is NOT a claim Spain has the highest online-shopping penetration or cheapest ads. Eurostat 2025: online buying among recent internet users Spain 73%, Germany 85%, France 84% (https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/statistics-explained/SEPDF/cache/46776.pdf). Spain CNMC reports 2025 ecommerce growth (https://cnmc.es/prensa/comercio-electronico-20260703); DHL Spain survey highlights delivery/returns and marketplace competition (https://www.dhl.com/content/dam/dhl/local/global/dhl-ecommerce/documents/pdf/g0-ecs-insights-country-report-spain-2025.pdf). Validate delivery times, VAT, Spanish consumer rights, 14-day withdrawal, product safety, ad CPA and fulfillment BEFORE launch. Prioritize Spain-fulfillable/EU-fulfillable compact, demonstrable products; no unverified 15-day China shipping promise. Gate alternative market only with comparable product-specific evidence and contribution economics.

## Execution phases — core 45 days + 4-day year-end extension
- Days 1–7 (Nov 13–19): research 15–25 product candidates; compare 5 finalists on demand evidence, competitive differentiation, landed cost, fulfillment risk, gross margin, ad suitability; choose 1–2.
- Days 8–14 (Nov 20–26): supplier due diligence; economics and pricing; landing/store MVP, analytics and consent-compliant tracking; 3 creative angles, checkout/payment readiness.
- Days 15–21 (Nov 27–Dec 3): QA funnel; create 3 e-commerce AI ads; test organically or with owner-approved ad budget; log impressions, CTR, CPC, add-to-cart, checkout, conversion, CPA and contribution margin.
- Days 22–30 (Dec 4–12): improve offer/landing/creative based on observed data; kill weak hypotheses; resolve fulfillment and support.
- Days 31–45 (Dec 13–27): concentrate on validated winner, increase budget only if economics and cash risk permit; monitor returns/refunds and delivery.
- Days 46–49 (Dec 28–31): year-end performance review, conversion/fulfillment optimization and final run-rate assessment; do not claim EUR 15k realized unless observed.
## Revenue math (illustrative, not projections)
EUR 15,000/month = EUR 500/day. At EUR 50 AOV: 300 orders/month (~10/day). At EUR 75 AOV: 200 orders/month (~6.7/day). At EUR 100 AOV: 150 orders/month (~5/day). Monitor contribution margin per order after product, fulfillment, processing, returns and acquisition; revenue alone is insufficient.
