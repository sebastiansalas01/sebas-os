## Five first-pass research priorities (NOT winners)

2. **K08 motion-sensor closet light:** strong demo; IKEA is an existing retail benchmark, not a supplier. Check EU electrical compliance, batteries, warranties and margins.
3. **K10 drill cleaning brush kit:** demonstrable use case, potentially compact. Validate attachment compatibility, packaging and saturation.
4. **K02 dog snuffle mat:** demonstrable enrichment; verify washable material, safety and supplier claims.
5. **K14 decorative peel-and-stick wall tiles:** strong transformation creative; validate adhesive, shipping damage and return costs.

## Minimum research for each finalist
1. Market and country selected; search-intent and competition evidence from at least two independent source types (e.g., actual competitor listing + ad library or dated demand signal).
2. At least two real suppliers with SKU URLs, MOQ, unit cost, shipping origin, lead time, EU/market compliance, return policy and sample option. **Not yet obtained.**
3. At least three dated competitor prices with URLs and screenshot/notes; no assumption that a category trend equals SKU demand.
4. Unit economics: selling price, discounts, COGS, freight, taxes/duties where applicable, processing fees, expected returns, estimated allowable CPA. If unknown, return `insufficient_data`.
5. Creative viability: 3 distinct ad angles, 1 landing offer hypothesis, and non-misleading product demonstration.
6. Reject counterfeit/IP-risk products and categories requiring unsupported health, medical or safety claims.

### Initial research sequence (no stock)
- **K01 pet hair remover**: Meta Ad Library ES search terms `quitapelos mascotas`, `rodillo quitapelos reutilizable`; TikTok Top Ads ES pets; compare 3 Spanish retail listings; identify two shipping-to-Spain supplier listings; verify cleaning performance and IP differentiation.
